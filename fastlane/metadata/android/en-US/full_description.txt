Spinet is an Android music player built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose.
It plays your own music from a folder on the phone, from a server you host, or
from sources you add yourself, with real background playback and lock-screen
controls. It comes with no sources of its own.

Features:

* Search across every enabled source, results merged.
* Background playback (Media3/ExoPlayer) with lock-screen and notification
  controls, and wake handling so audio keeps playing when the screen locks.
* On-device music: point the app at a phone folder and play your files offline.
* Your own servers: add several, in the order you want them used. Browse folders as playlists, upload from the phone,
  save to the server, and delete, on a backend you host yourself.
* Downloads with title, artist and cover art embedded, so tracks survive offline.
* Plays the copy closest to you first: the phone, then your server, then online.
* Duplicate-free library; playlists never hold the same song twice.
* Playlists: create, import from CSV, toggle drag-to-reorder, and pick
  alternate versions of a song (with preview before replacing).
* Shuffle, repeat-all and repeat-one, with a currently-playing indicator and a
  progress bar on every list.
* Themes, including custom themes from hex colours.
* Session resume: remembers your queue, track, position and shuffle/repeat state.
